# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static `index.html` + CSS + vanilla JS + Three.js (user's choice). Three.js and fonts vendored locally so the site runs offline on the instructor's laptop; phones load it by QR over mobile data. Hosting target: open decision (GitHub Pages proposed).

## Users

1. **Workshop participants (primary)**: 10–15 people at FICPA 2026 (Festival Internacional de Cine de Pasto): filmmakers, screenwriters, art/design students, gamers. In a classroom, no laptops, some phones. They see the site projected and open it on their phones via QR during a single ~4.5 h session.
2. **Instructor (operator)**: Andrés Ordóñez-Bolaños runs the site from one laptop connected to a projector, as a live companion for the session (timer, mission objectives, plan scanner, premiere).
3. **After the festival**: participants, FICPA organizers and the wider public use it as the workshop's memory and gallery.

## Product Purpose

Companion and legacy site for the workshop **GALERAS 2037 — Del plano al mundo jugable**. It makes the workshop's thesis tangible: a paper level map ("plano") becomes a walkable 3D world, and that world becomes a place to film shots ("planos"). Success: participants watch their own paper map turn into a navigable dystopian Pasto and leave with a QR link to their team's outcomes.

## Positioning

Transfers AAA game design, level design and technical-leadership method to cinema, applied to a dystopian or transhumanist Pasto in 2037. The territory (Galeras volcano, Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, barniz de Pasto / mopa-mopa, La Cocha) works as a system, not as scenery. No programming is required from participants.

## Operating Context

- Single in-person session (date within 13–17 Oct 2026, mornings), one projector and one laptop. Venue wifi may be missing.
- Paper-first workshop: printed kit (world cards, level-map grid templates, storyboards, playtest sheets). Phones are used to shoot short clips and photo sequences.
- Teams produce an "Expandable Scene Package" (Paquete de Escena Expandible) plus a 30–45 s microteaser, photo sequence or animatic.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must run fully offline on the laptop; must be light enough for mid-range Android phones.
- Interactive Three.js world: intro scene, a scanner that turns a photographed grid map into a 3D level, first-person walk, and a virtual camera that can record clips.
- Gallery content comes only from real workshop outputs (to be added after the session).
- Undecided: final hosting/URL, exact session date, and the instructor's credits wording (Andrés will dictate the exact phrase; do not reuse the proposal's company list without confirmation).

## Product Principles

1. The experience comes before the tool. Every interactive element must teach a concept from the workshop.
2. Paper first, digital as amplifier. Everything the site does has an analog equivalent in the room.
3. Pasto is a system, not a backdrop.
4. AI assists and authorship decides. AI use is optional and declared.
5. Make it work offline before making it spectacular.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mixed digital literacy. Projected text must be readable from the back of a room. Phone views need large touch targets. Nothing in the workshop may depend on owning a device.
