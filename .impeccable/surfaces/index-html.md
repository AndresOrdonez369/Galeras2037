## Direction contract

THESIS: Cyberpunk del Sur. Pasto 2037 seen from its night market: hand-painted rótulos, sun-bleached tarps and pirate LED tickers under Galeras ash. It refuses the imported neon-on-black cyberpunk skyline.

OWN-WORLD: Dark ash ground (#1E1D1B) with ash-white paper (#D9D3C4). Tarp blue #1F5FA8 and tarp orange #F26B1D own whole regions. Rótulo yellow #F2C230 and rótulo red #C8261E are painted signals. Display: Bungee / Bungee Shade (urban signage, painted drop shade). LED layer: Doto dot-matrix, only for tickers and system states. Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible for projection. Corrugated zinc, tarp and paper are the materials. No glass, no gradient text, no cards.

STORY: The visitor arrives in the market, understands that every city starts as a plan, watches the plan become a world, and goes to the slides or the workshop route. It feels like the workshop is a level they are about to play.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL street: tarped stalls on both sides, cables overhead, the Galeras glowing at the vanishing point as the landmark, ash falling. GALERAS 2037 is painted as a giant rótulo on a zinc sheet hanging across the street. An LED ticker crawls along the bottom with transmission lines. Actions "Entrar a las slides" and "Recorrer el taller" are painted on tarp plates, bottom left. Scrolling lifts the camera to top-down and flattens the city into the A3 paper map with the workshop legend, then it re-extrudes.

FORM: Assigned direction 6 of my grounded list (Rótulo y Lona). Seed key dc5f1556. Raises: staged zone thresholds (deco lobby); states printed as LED transmissions (phosphor); controls named with real film/physical terms (ink basin); one continuous tension-curve line threads the page and the deck progress (provenance).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
